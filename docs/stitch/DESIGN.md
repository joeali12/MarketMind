## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, tranquil, and dependable SaaS ecosystem tailored for small-to-medium retail owners, merchants, and operations leads across Cairo, Alexandria, and the wider MENA landscape. The visual identity bridges institutional-grade data intelligence with intuitive, welcoming commerce clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Calm & Grounded:** Data is presented without hyperbolic visual noise, artificial neon glows, or pseudo-futuristic hype. It acts as a trusted senior business partner.
- **Approachable Sophistication:** Built for independent boutique proprietors, supermarket managers, and multi-branch retail directors who need instantaneous clarity without technical steepness.
- **Bi-Directional Harmony:** Thoughtfully balanced to maintain identical visual weight, rhythm, and clarity across both English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) surfaces.

### Visual Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Humanist Corporate** with **Soft Scandinavian Minimalism**:
- Generous breathable margins and cool, mist-tinted canvas backdrops.
- Expressive, pillowy corner radii (18px to 24px) that soften analytical density and eliminate spreadsheet fatigue.
- Pure optical white cards floating quietly above muted cool-gray canvas planes, bound by hairline borders and feathered ambient illumination.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, low-fatigue workspace optimized for daytime store management, inventory audits, and sales reconciliation.

### Roles & Applications
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F8FA` / `#F3F4F6`):** Cool off-white canvas that eliminates glare while maintaining high legibility under varying shop-floor ambient light.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white applied to data cards, modals, and sheets to provide clear separation from the canvas.
- **Primary Brand (`#4F46E5` & `#4338CA`):** Restrained deep indigo used purposefully for primary calls-to-action, key performance highlights, and active segment selections.
- **Soft Indigo Wash (`#EEF2FF`):** Tinted container background for subtle callouts, active tab surfaces, and selected item states.
- **Slate Secondary / Dark Pills (`#18181B` & `#1F2937`):** Deep charcoal-slate applied to selected navigation indicators, primary metric values, and prominent badge markers.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - *Positive Delta / Growth (`#10B981` with `#ECFDF5` container):* Authentic emerald green for upward profit shifts, positive cash flow, and healthy stock levels.
  - *Warning / Review Required (`#F59E0B` with `#FEF3C7` container):* Balanced amber for expiring inventory, payment follow-ups, and anomaly alerts.
  - *Error / Negative Delta (`#EF4444` with `#FEF2F2` container):* Precise coral red for churn risk, revenue drop, and out-of-stock notices.
- **Typography Neutrals:**
  - *Primary Headings (`#0F172A` / `#111827`):* Ink-rich obsidian for effortless scan-reading.
  - *Secondary Text & Metadata (`#4B5563` / `#6B7280`):* Neutral slate providing balanced hierarchy without vanishing into the background.
  - *Hairline Structural Dividers (`#E5E7EB` / `#EAECF0`):* Boundary borders for tables, cards, and segmented pills.

## Typography

The type scale combines crisp Swiss geometric structure with localized Arabic legibility.

### Font System & Bi-Directionality
- **Latin Typography:** Powered by `Inter`, supplemented by native system sans-serif stacks (`-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`) for instantaneous loading on low-bandwidth retail connections.
- **Arabic Typography:** Paired cleanly with `IBM Plex Sans Arabic` and `Noto Sans Arabic`. Arabic letterforms require slightly increased vertical leading (`+2px` on body and headline levels) to accommodate ascenders and diacritical marks gracefully.
- **Numbers & Currencies:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced on all financial tables, metrics, and EGP (جم) values to ensure strict vertical alignment across rows.

## Layout & Spacing

A predictable, breathable layout system built to display dense transactional tables and high-level analytical KPI grids side by side without clutter.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max-width canvas container of `1440px` centered with fluid margins (`2rem`), dynamic 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Cards re-flow into dual-column layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and gutters. Horizontal scroll is applied to analytics filter pills and dense tabular reports.

### Rhythm & Vertical Cadence
- Macro component separation adheres to `space-xl` (`2rem`) and `space-2xl` (`3rem`), preventing cognitive overload for non-technical users.
- Micro component interiors (card padding, list item gaps) strictly leverage `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile physical boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a calm, premium desktop atmosphere.

### Depth Philosophy
1. **Canvas (Ground Level 0):** Muted cool-gray (`#F7F8FA`) forms the substrate.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1):** White cards (`#FFFFFF`) defined by a hairline structural border (`1px solid #EAECF0`) and dual-stage micro-shadows:
   - *Rest Shadow:* `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
   - *Hover Lift Shadow:* `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05), 0 16px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Level 2):** Modals, dropdown popovers, and sticky header bars leverage high-diffusion blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and a focused shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design language uses generous, friendly curvature to eliminate the intimidating, rigid aesthetic of classic enterprise software.

### Corner Radius System
- **Cards & Data Containers:** `18px` to `24px` (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`), creating a warm, organic frame around data tables and charts.
- **Controls & Navigation:** Full pill contours (`9999px`) for segmented navigation bars, badge indicators, chip filters, and actionable buttons.
- **Input Fields & Dropdowns:** `12px` to `16px` for accessible touch/click targets that integrate smoothly with pill-shaped triggers.

## Components

### Top Navigation Bar
- A fixed, floating horizontal header across desktop screens with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` background and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Brand Unit:** Distinctive geometric logomark paired with ink-slate brand title.
- **Navigation Cluster:** Centered segment containing full-pill tabs (*Dashboard*, *Products*, *Customers*, *Insights*, *Upload*). Unselected items render in `#4B5563` with `#F3F4F6` hover; active tab renders in dark obsidian slate (`#18181B`) with crisp white text.
- **Contextual Utilities:**
  - *Data Source Badge:* Pill capsule showing file status (`File: Cairo_Retail_Q3.csv · EGP`) in `#F3F4F6` with subtle green live indicator dot.
  - *Language Switcher:* Segmented pill switch (`EN` | `عربي`) allowing instant RTL/LTR transformation without full-page disorientation.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** High-saturation indigo (`#4F46E5`), full pill radius, horizontal padding of `1.5rem`, label weight 600. Active click produces subtle micro-scale compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Crisp white background with `#D1D5DB` hairline border and slate typography (`#1F2937`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost Buttons:** Transparent surface with interactive hover tint (`#EEF2FF`).

### Metric & KPI Cards
- White card background with `20px` corner radius and `1.5rem` internal padding.
- Metric hierarchy: Small muted label (`label-md`), followed by high-contrast numerical metric (`stat-metric`), anchored by a pill-shaped delta badge at the base:
  - *Positive:* Soft mint green container (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#059669`), paired with an upward trend arrow (`↑ +14.2% vs last month`).
  - *Warning/Review:* Pale amber container (`#FEF3C7`), dark amber text (`#D97706`).

### Input Fields & Data Selectors
- Rounded inputs (`12px` to `14px`) with `#F9FAFB` fill and `#E5E7EB` border.
- On focus: Background shifts to `#FFFFFF`, surrounded by a 3px indigo focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`) with `#4F46E5` primary border.
- RTL alignment mirrors prefixes, currency tokens (e.g., `EGP` or `ج.م`), and clear icons automatically.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- **Checkboxes:** `6px` radius squares with smooth slate borders; checked state fills with `#4F46E5` displaying a bold white check icon.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) with `0.5rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding. Selected chips feature `#18181B` fill with pure white typography.

### Data Tables
- Header row uses uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`) in `#6B7280` over transparent or cool-wash rows.
- Alternating row zebra patterns are avoided; distinction is maintained through clean horizontal hairline dividers (`#F3F4F6`) and gentle `#F9FAFB` hover states.
